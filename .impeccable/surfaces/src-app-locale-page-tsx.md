## Scope

The whole public site: `/[locale]` (sheet 01) and `/[locale]/work/[slug]` (sheets 02–04), in English, Portuguese and Spanish.

## Visitor mode

Persuade. The visitor is a business owner or operations lead deciding, within minutes and often in a second language, whether Ryan can be trusted to build the system their company will run on. Success is one qualified email to ryan.voltzagency@gmail.com.

## Audience, job, action

- **Audience:** buyers of custom business systems in retail, education and professional services, plus recruiters and agency partners scanning for stack fit.
- **Job:** judge credibility and delivery ownership fast, then find a way to make contact.
- **Action:** email (primary), WhatsApp / LinkedIn / GitHub (secondary), open a live client site (evidence).

## Proof and content

Eight live systems with public addresses, three of them with their own sheet. No testimonials, no figures, no logos beyond the projects themselves. Screenshots are real captures of the live sites.
